ABOUT

A little about me

I’m a geography and GIS student interested in using spatial data, programming, and field experience to understand real-world environmental and infrastructure problems.

MY PATH

How I got into GIS

My path into GIS grew out of an interest in the outdoors, environmental work, and understanding how places function. Before focusing on GIS, I spent several years working in field-based roles where location, landscape, and environmental conditions were a major part of the job.

Those experiences made me increasingly interested in the spatial side of the work: how geographic data could be collected, organized, analyzed, and used to answer practical questions.

That eventually led me to study geography and GIS at the University of Minnesota. Since then, I’ve become especially interested in spatial analysis, Python automation, interactive mapping, and the ways GIS can support environmental and infrastructure decision-making.
