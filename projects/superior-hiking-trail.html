MAPBOX

Interactive Superior Hiking Trail Basemap

A custom Mapbox basemap of Minnesota's Superior Hiking Trail, designed to give users a high level overview of terrain, campsites, trailheads, and resupply towns.

Tools

Mapbox

Project Type

Cartography & Web Mapping

Study Area

Superior Hiking Trail, Minnesota

Overview

The Superior Hiking Trail runs nearly 300 miles along the North Shore of Lake Superior. I grew up hiking and backpacking on the trail. As much as I love my guidebook for the trail, I've always dreamed of having an interactive map with all this information.
